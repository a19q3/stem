# Four-replica payment pressure and storage aging

Four native Jolteon replicas at `fefb8cc` ran on six physical cores with SMT. The 64-thread load generator used two other physical cores on the same workstation. A separate four-replica 4 KiB aging trial occupied the other eight cores. The trials shared disks, memory and the host. Each measured payment spent a different output of an actual signed funding transaction. Before starting the timer, the harness located every funding transaction in the common chain and compared the same Header on every node. The larger fixture finalized 3,750 splits with 960,000 outputs, the smaller 2,110 splits with 540,000 outputs. Three replicas retained a fixed history window and the fourth archived history.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3,000/s for 180 s | 327,306 / 327,306 / 327,306 | 293,336 (1,629.64/s) | 173.667 s | 34,063 |
| 1,600/s for 600 s | 726,442 / 726,303 / 726,324 | 724,901 (1,208.17/s) | 3.507 s | 2,566 |

The generator dropped 212,694 and 233,558 scheduled payments before dispatch. The three-minute trial recorded no failed writes or missing acknowledgements. The ten-minute trial had 139 socket-response errors, including 21 transactions later observed in the common chain. No submitted payment returned a missing-input error. Every successful acknowledgement in each run reached common visibility. The ten individual minute windows ranged from 1,154 to 1,272 common confirmations per second. These measurements do not establish 3,000 sustained TPS. Linux attributed 11,153,162,240 and 35,704,524,800 device write bytes to the four replica scopes over the respective measured windows. RocksDB recorded 28 and 137 compaction jobs producing output over the full fixtures, including the funding stage. All SST bytes were captured and decoded. The ten-minute fixture produced 9,977,074,969 flush bytes and 19,777,150,590 compaction bytes. Of the latter, 14,558,435,866 encoded bytes belonged to Cell-trie blocks and 3,953,572,069 to Live Cell blocks. The reports separately account for mixed blocks and metadata. Shared filesystem writes and drive-internal NAND writes have no unique namespace attribution in these counters. ## One hour with 4 KiB payment chains

This four-replica fixture circulated 8,192 payment chains at 100 transactions/s for 3,600 seconds. Each carried 4,096 bytes. The load generator used 32 senders on cores 0–1,16–17, with replicas on cores 2–7,18–23. Separate pressure experiments shared the host and disks during parts of the hour. The six disjoint intervals below group transactions by the time they first became visible to every replica. The p99 measures from socket write to common visibility for transactions observed in that interval.

The client wrote 357,964 of 360,000 scheduled transactions and received 309,007 successful acknowledgements. Four replicas agreed on 309,011 transactions through height 12,073. The common chain contained 4 transactions without a successful acknowledgement. 0 acknowledged transactions were absent from that chain. Within the offer window, 308,959 were visible on every replica. The whole-run write-to-common p99 was 1.924 s, and the largest acknowledged backlog was 340. The first and last ten-minute windows delivered 97.75 and 79.47 common TPS. The signed payment dependency graph explains the falling confirmation rate. In 1,771 chains the first gap was a client drop before the socket write (996 queue-full, 775 deadline). All 48,953 Live Cell rejections were descendants of such a gap. Another 265 descendants were dropped by the client. Every payment in the other 6,421 chains finalized. The client received four socket errors for payments that also finalized. The experiment consequently measures storage under a declining successful workload. [First-gap counts](one-hour-chains.json) · [Replay the dependency check](chains.py)

Linux attributed 34,809,257,984 block-device write bytes to the four replica scopes, 1.84 device bytes per serialized WriteBatch byte. The collector decoded all 414 SST files, including 2,229,801,384 flush bytes and 2,951,444,308 compaction-output bytes. All 79 recorded compaction jobs reconcile by file, namespace and LSM level. They read 2,192,695,163 bytes at level 0 and 2,725,533,921 at level 6. All compaction output went to level 6. The repeated 4 KiB content in this fixture compresses readily.
