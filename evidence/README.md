# Local execution evidence

- [Custody](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-custody.json.xz) contains native, xUDT and legacy round-trip runs from source commit `edc2df6bf879463b45d7657f942e2613e8cd8de8`. The native run returned 1,199.98 CKB, rejected a tampered withdrawal nonce and verified that a deep L1 reorg stops approvals. The xUDT run deposited 1,200 base units and 1,199.99 CKB, withdrew 500 base units and 850 CKB, and verified 700 units remained in escrow. The legacy run verified an existing receipt format with the custodian's replica.
- [Spore V2](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-spore.json.xz) contains the transactions and proofs for mint, transfer and melt across the four replicas, plus the VM rejection of content mutation.
- [systemd recovery](2026-11-25-systemd.json) records the four-validator service run. After killing one validator's application, the monitored app and CometBFT processes restarted and all four replicas observed the second payment in 4.552 seconds. A subsequent coordinated stop and start preserved access to the third payment.
- [Operator regression](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-operators.json.xz) records a successful weighted committee handoff, checkpoint sync with a restart, and the xUDT round trip through the replay-safe mint command. The `handoff` and `mint_relay` objects each contain their public `committee` and `execution_proof`.
- [Wallet RPC and contract compatibility](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-wallet.json.xz) records the stock CCC wallet payment and index rebuild, the Spore V2 lifecycle through the CKB RPC gateway, the successful `data2` CellScript spend and rejected witness, and the live Cell index check after checkpoint sync and restart. Use `wallet.execution_proof` and `cellscript.execution_proof` with their respective `committee` objects. Use `spore.proofs.minted`, `spore.proofs.transferred`, and `spore.proofs.melted` with `spore.committee`. `indexed_snapshot` contains the recovery timings and verification result.
- [Indexed systemd recovery](2026-11-25-systemd-wallet.json) records a crash and subsequent coordinated restart of the indexed validator. The application and CometBFT restarted, all replicas saw the payment submitted during the outage in 4.657 seconds, and the recovered replica answered indexed queries for Cells committed before and after the crash.
- [Prepared withdrawals](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-prepared-payouts.json.xz) records native CKB, xUDT and legacy-receipt releases built by `bridge prepare-payout`. Each scenario includes the unsigned and signed L1 transactions and the checked and committed L1 hash. The `native`, `xudt`, and `legacy` objects each contain an `execution_proof` and its public `committee`.
- [Live Cell checkpoints](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-cell-proofs.json.xz) contains a checkpoint membership proof rebuilt after P2P snapshot restore and restart, plus a proof after a weighted committee handoff. Extract `snapshot.cell_proof` or `handoff.cell_proof` and its corresponding `committee`. The matching `result.live_cell_proof.verified` records the height and packed OutPoint for the `-cell-proof` verifier. The archive also records rejection of a modified Cell, altered Merkle sibling, changed manifest, incorrect OutPoint and different chain. `legacy_snapshot.result` records the format-1 regression run.
- [Quorum and admission regressions](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-quorum-admission.json.xz) records the weight-four versus weight-two partition. Both groups held at height 2, and both admitted payments reached all four replicas 4.149 seconds after restoring the links. Verify `partition.proofs.two_thirds` and `partition.proofs.one_third` with `partition.committee`. The archive also records a systemd crash recovery with three admission workers and the prepared xUDT payout regression under the same binary. The typed withdrawal proof is `xudt_regression.proof` with `xudt_regression.committee`.
- [Compact checkpoint recovery](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-compact-recovery.json.xz) records archive and compact validators agreeing on a checkpoint, preserved committed status after restart and local pruning, and a post-sync spend with a relative `since` and historical header dependency. `v3.cell_proof` and `v3.spend_proof` use `v3.committee`. `governance` records the format-3 weighted handoff and full retained governance transaction. `xudt` and `xudt_legacy` contain bridge round trips and snapshot restores with authenticated and legacy withdrawal receipts. Each contains `execution_proof` and its public `committee`.
- [Live Cell replay protection](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-live-replay.json.xz) records format-4 archive and compact validators agreeing on a checkpoint, an ordinary replay rejected after restore, wallet status after a post-restore spend, and the retained weighted committee handoff. The `xudt` and `xudt_legacy` runs preserve the bridge mint and withdrawal records through state sync. Verify each `execution_proof` with the corresponding `committee`. The `snapshot`, `governance` and `format_2` objects also contain `cell_proof` and the expected height and OutPoint under `result.live_cell_proof.verified`. `format_1` records the full-archive state-sync regression.
- [Execution proofs without a CometBFT transaction index](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-comet-index.json.xz) contains proofs exported from a pruned payment replica, after a weighted committee handoff, and for the xUDT withdrawal accepted by the local CKB vault. Verify each `execution_proof` with its `committee`. `snapshot.altered_provider` records that modified transaction bytes and execution results were rejected against the captured signed headers.
- [L1 custody migration](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-vault-rotation.json.xz) records native CKB and xUDT migrations to disjoint custodian groups with authenticated and legacy withdrawal receipts. Verify the post-rotation `execution_proof` with the corresponding `committee`. `l1_signed_transactions` contains each approved rotation and payout, and `result.custody_migration` contains the L1 headers and transaction and witness proofs checked on the dev chain. The native run reopened deposits after rotation. The xUDT run released a pending typed withdrawal after the new multisig took control. The native reorg check took place after the inclusion proofs were collected.
- [Deposit guardian rotation](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-guardian-rotation.json.xz) records a disjoint guardian handoff and a same-key refresh on format-4 native bridges with authenticated and legacy withdrawals. The compact observer restored the active policy, the committed L1 backing anchor and both signed transitions. Verify each `native.proofs` or `native_legacy.proofs` bundle against the corresponding `committee`. The native scenario also records old and relabelled certificate rejection, retirement of an outdated anchor context, idempotent lookup of a previously finalized mint, and a fresh journal refusing to attest after the committed L1 backing anchor was reorganized. `fixed_guardian_xudt` and `validator_handoff` record regressions on the same binary.
- [Rolling application upgrade](https://github.com/a19q3/stem/blob/12338f2fea73c8df437694da473d08e950a0938b/evidence/2026-11-25-rolling-upgrade.json.xz) records four compatible upgrades and a rollback followed by re-upgrade on the 3, 3, 2, 2 committee. Remaining validators certified payments during each stop. The restarted validator replayed those results and advanced its original signing state. The 2, 2, 1, 1 run refused both weight-2 restarts and exercised the compatible change on the lighter validators. For either rollout, verify the bundles in `proofs` with its `committee` and replay the quorum checks from `report.steps[].preflight`. `previously_rotated_committee` carries a verified handoff and `pending_committee` carries the certificate that refuses an upgrade at the height immediately before activation.

Run `xz -dc FILE.json.xz` to view the JSON. Custody proofs use `native.execution_proof`, `xudt.execution_proof` and `legacy.execution_proof`, with their corresponding `committee` objects. The Spore proofs use `proofs.minted`, `proofs.transferred`, and `proofs.melted` with the top-level `committee` object. Save the selected JSON objects separately and pass them to `stem-proof -genesis COMMITTEE.json -proof PROOF.json`. The bundled committee keys identify the recorded development network.
